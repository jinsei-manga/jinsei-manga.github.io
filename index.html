Jinsei Manga Uploader

Jinsei Manga Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "あの日の選択【人生漫画】" (https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC95h8t9cWulWpbrideCS0rg) to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

このツールは、YouTubeチャンネル「あの日の選択【人生漫画】」の運営者本人が、自分で制作した動画を自分のチャンネルにアップロード・予約するためだけに使う非公開のツールです。

  • Data accessed: the tool only uses the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload videos, set thumbnails and set the publish time on the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or share any other YouTube or Google user data.
